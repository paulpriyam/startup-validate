# India Customer Discovery

How to validate problems and solutions with Indian customers.

## The Indian Interview Context
- **Trust Barrier:** Indian customers may be polite but skeptical. Use warm intros or "insider" language.
- **Language Comfort:** Conduct interviews in the customer's preferred language (Hindi, Tamil, Hinglish, etc.) if possible.
- **WhatsApp first:** Many discovery conversations in India happen via WhatsApp voice notes or quick chats.
- **Offline to Online:** If targeting SMBs/Kiranas, you MUST visit in person.

## Interview Protocol
1. **Problem Exploration:** "How do you currently handle X?" "What was the last time X caused a problem?"
2. **Behavioral Evidence:** "Have you paid for a solution? Did you build a workaround?"
3. **Willingness to Pay:** "If this cost ₹X per month, how would that affect your decision?"

## India-Specific Experiments
- **WhatsApp Concierge:** Run the service manually via WhatsApp before building an app.
- **UPI Intent Test:** Ask a customer to pay a small "commitment fee" via UPI to validate intent.
- **Kirana Pilot:** Place a physical sign or QR code in a local shop to test footfall interest.
- **Regional Content Test:** Run ads in local languages to test click-through rates.
