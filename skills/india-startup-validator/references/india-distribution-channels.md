# India Distribution Channels

Evaluating how to reach the Indian customer.

## Core Channels

### 1. WhatsApp & Community
- **WhatsApp Groups:** Niche groups for traders, apartments, parents, or professionals.
- **WhatsApp Channels/Status:** For broadcast and engagement.
- **Community Leaders:** Influencing the "admin" or head of a local association.

### 2. Digital Platforms
- **Instagram/YouTube:** Essential for B2C and Creator-led brands in India.
- **LinkedIn:** For B2B/Enterprise in Metro cities.
- **ONDC:** The open network for e-commerce, food, and mobility.
- **Google Maps/Local SEO:** Critical for "near me" services.

### 3. Physical & Channel Networks
- **Kirana / MSME Networks:** Reaching customers through local retail touchpoints.
- **Reseller Models:** Leveraging local individuals (e.g., Meesho model) to sell to their circles.
- **Field Sales:** Necessary for high-ACV SMB or Enterprise deals in India.
- **Campus Ambassadors:** Reaching the Gen Z / Student market.

### 4. Partnerships
- **SaaS/B2B:** Partnering with Tally, Khatabook, or Zoho for SMB access.
- **Financial:** Bank or NBFC partnerships for fintech distribution.
- **Government:** Tapping into state-level schemes or CSC (Common Service Centres).
