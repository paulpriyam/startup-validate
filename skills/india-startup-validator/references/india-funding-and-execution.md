# India Funding and Execution

Use this reference to assess whether the idea can be built and financed in the Indian context. Treat current program rules, funding data, and eligibility requirements as time-sensitive and verify them against official sources.

## Financing Paths

- **Bootstrapping and revenue-first:** Assess whether the founder can reach a useful first version with services, pilots, pre-orders, or customer-funded implementation.
- **DPIIT / Startup India:** Check whether recognition, grants, incubator access, or public programs are relevant. Do not imply eligibility without verification.
- **Incubators and accelerators:** Identify sector and geography fit, equity terms, program quality, and actual distribution access rather than treating acceptance as validation.
- **Angels and seed funds:** Compare the idea with India-specific investor theses, stage expectations, traction requirements, and category appetite.
- **Strategic partnerships:** Consider banks, NBFCs, SaaS platforms, schools, hospitals, associations, distributors, and enterprise buyers as potential financing or distribution partners.

## Execution Checks

- Can the founder test the idea without raising capital?
- Does the model require expensive field operations, local-language support, collections, or compliance staff?
- Is the initial city and customer segment narrow enough for direct learning?
- Can early revenue fund the next validation step?
- Does the plan depend on investor funding before proving demand?
- Are government, enterprise, or procurement sales cycles longer than the runway?

## Red Flags

- The plan assumes fundraising is the first validation signal.
- The model needs high volume before contribution margin becomes positive.
- A grant or accelerator is treated as guaranteed.
- The company needs licenses, partnerships, or certifications before it can test the core problem.

## Output Requirement

State whether the idea is best suited to bootstrapping, pilots, customer-funded services, grants, angel funding, institutional funding, or a staged combination. Label funding assumptions `[Assumption]` and verify current schemes and terms before making decisions.
