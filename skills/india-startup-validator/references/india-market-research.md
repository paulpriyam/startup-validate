# India Market Research

Guidelines for researching the Indian startup ecosystem.

## India-Specific Data Sources
- **DPIIT / Startup India:** For sector trends and government recognition data.
- **NPCI / UPI Statistics:** For digital payment adoption and transaction volumes.
- **IAMAI / Reports:** For internet and smartphone penetration data.
- **MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs):** For competitor filing signals (if available).
- **Tracxn / Entrackr / YourStory:** For funding and local ecosystem news.
- **ONDC Portal:** For e-commerce and logistics trends.
- **Local Review Sites:** MouthShut, Play Store (India-specific reviews), Google Maps.

## Market Sizing (India Context)
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** Total potential customers in India for the category.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** The segment within TAM you can actually reach (e.g., English-speaking smartphone users).
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** What you can capture in the next 12-24 months (e.g., Metro city professionals).

## Research Principles
- **Quantify in INR:** Convert all financial claims to INR for local context.
- **Tier-wise Analysis:** Distinguish between Metro (Tier 1), Tier 2, and Tier 3 behaviors.
- **Regulatory Overlay:** Always check if a trend is driven or blocked by policy (e.g., Data Localization, DPDP Act).
- **Identify WhatsApp Substitutes:** Many Indian "solutions" are just organized WhatsApp groups. Research these as competitors.
