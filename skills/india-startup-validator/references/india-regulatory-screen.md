# India Regulatory Screen

A checklist for identifying regulatory hurdles in the Indian market.

## Sector-Specific Checks

### Fintech
- **RBI Guidelines:** Digital lending, PPI licenses, Payment Aggregator rules.
- **NPCI:** UPI integration rules and transaction limits.
- **KYC/AML:** Aadhaar-based KYC, Video KYC, and anti-money laundering compliance.
- **Account Aggregator:** Consent-based data sharing framework.

### Healthtech
- **Telemedicine Guidelines:** Rules for online consultations and prescriptions.
- **DISHA / Health Data:** Digital Information Security in Healthcare Act (draft) and ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) compliance.
- **Pharmacy:** E-pharmacy regulations and FSSAI (for nutraceuticals).

### Edtech
- **ASC / Advertising:** Guidelines for making claims about results/placements.
- **Data Privacy:** Special protections for children's data under DPDP Act.

### E-commerce / Consumer
- **Consumer Protection Rules:** Rules for e-commerce entities, including inventory vs marketplace models.
- **ONDC:** Open Network for Digital Commerce protocols.
- **FSSAI:** Mandatory for food-related businesses.

### General Business
- **DPDP Act 2023:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act compliance (consent, storage, localization).
- **GST:** Thresholds for registration and invoicing requirements.
- **Shops & Establishments:** Local state-level commercial licensing.
- **DPIIT Recognition:** Benefits for registered startups (tax breaks, patent fast-tracking).

## Disclaimer
*This screen is for strategic decision-making only and does not constitute legal advice. Always consult with a qualified Indian legal professional.*
