# India Unit Economics

Benchmarks and logic for Indian startup economics.

## Typical INR Price Bands
- **B2C Mass:** ₹49 - ₹199 / month (High volume, low ARPU).
- **B2C Premium:** ₹499 - ₹1999 / month.
- **SMB:** ₹500 - ₹5000 / month.
- **Enterprise:** ₹50k+ / month.

## Cost Factors (India)
- **Payment Gateway:** 2% - 3% (UPI often lower/free for small txns).
- **Cloud/Infrastructure:** Global rates, often a high % of INR revenue.
- **Operations/Support:** Lower than West, but scales with volume.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Highly variable. Instagram/FB in India is cheaper but conversion varies.

## Critical Ratios
- **LTV/CAC:** Should aim for 3x+ in the long run.
- **Payback Period:** Aim for < 6-8 months for B2C, < 12 months for SMB.
- **Gross Margin:** Aim for 60%+ for SaaS, 15-20% for Marketplaces.

## Red Flags
- **Negative Contribution Margin:** Losing money on every transaction.
- **High Churn in Low-ARPU:** If it costs ₹100 to get a ₹49 customer who leaves in a month.
- **Collection Risk:** High dependence on manual follow-ups for payment.
