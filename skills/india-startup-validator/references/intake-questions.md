# Intake Questions

Ask these questions in a conversational flow. Don't ask all at once.

## 1. Idea & Problem
- What is the core problem you are solving?
- Who feels this pain most acutely in India?
- How do they solve it today? (WhatsApp, spreadsheets, manual, etc.)

## 2. Target Market
- Which specific city/tier are you targeting first? (e.g., Metro, Tier 2, Tier 3)
- What is the primary language of your target customer?
- Is this a B2C, B2B, or SMB-focused solution?

## 3. Business & Economics
- How do you plan to make money?
- What is your target price point in INR?
- Do you have any early traction or customer conversations?

## 4. Risks & Constraints
- Are you in a regulated sector (Fintech, Health, etc.)?
- What is your biggest concern about this idea right now?
- What are your time and budget constraints for validation?

## 5. Distribution
- How do you plan to reach your first 100 customers?
- Do you have an unfair advantage in reaching this audience?
