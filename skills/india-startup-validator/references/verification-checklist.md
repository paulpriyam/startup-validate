# Verification Checklist

The final audit for the India Startup Validator.

## Universal Checks
- [ ] Every major claim is labeled (**[Data]**, **[Estimate]**, **[Assumption]**, etc.).
- [ ] No generic marketing fluff or cheerleading.
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are noted.
- [ ] Data age is flagged if older than 18 months.

## Skill-Specific Checks
- [ ] **India Specificity:** Does the analysis mention city tiers, local languages, or local payment/regulations?
- [ ] **Regulatory Caveats:** Is there a clear disclaimer that this is NOT legal advice?
- [ ] **Pricing in INR:** Are all financial projections and benchmarks in Indian Rupees?
- [ ] **Kill Criteria:** Are there 3-5 specific conditions that would make the founder stop?
- [ ] **Evidence Gaps:** Are the "We don't know yet" areas clearly listed?

## Final Dashboard Check
- [ ] Does the Scorecard match the evidence in the research files?
- [ ] Is the 30-day plan actionable and specific (e.g., "Message 20 people on LinkedIn")?
